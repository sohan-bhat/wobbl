Assumptions

  1. LiCoO2 cell at 100% charge (Moli ICR18650), 18 by 65 mm, with the kinetics of Hu, He, Rein and Restuccia, Numerical Study of Self-Heating Ignition of a Box of Lithium-Ion Batteries During Storage, Fire Technology 2020, Table 2, from Hatchard et al. 2001 and Kim et al. 2007.
  2. Cooled by 5 W/(m2 K) of convection and radiation at an emissivity of 0.8 over its whole surface, in surroundings taken from the hottest point of the heat study in ../battery-pack/output.
  3. The cell is lumped, open circuit and at rest, with no internal short, overcharge or outside fire. Runaway is the moment the cell heats itself faster than 10 C/min, the trigger Feng et al. (Frontiers in Energy Research 2018) use.
  4. The kinetics describe a fully charged LiCoO2 cell. LFP cells run away at higher temperatures and NMC cells at similar ones, so for other chemistries treat the answer as indicative.
  5. The kinetics carry the cell up to the trigger. What follows it, the separator melting, venting and fire, is beyond them, so the temperatures after the trigger are not a forecast.
