Suggested fixes:
  - Print in an enclosure near 45 C: The locked in shrinkage runs from the glass transition down to the air around the part, so air at 45 C instead of 25 C cuts the warp to about 2.86 mm.
  - Print it in PETG: PETG locks in 0.33% against 0.72% here, for about 1.75 mm of warp, if its strength and heat resistance suit the part.
  - Add a brim and keep the bed hot: While it prints a corner pulls up with about 336 N. A 5 to 8 mm brim gives that corner more bed to hold, and a bed near 100 C keeps the first layers soft and stuck, which this model assumes; it does not change the warp after release.
