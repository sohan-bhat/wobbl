Assumptions

  1. Links 300 mm ground, 80 mm crank, 300 mm coupler and 110 mm rocker, a crank rocker linkage, flat and stacked so the links never touch.
  2. The rocker's load is a drag of 6 N m against its motion, as from a wiper blade or a door.
  3. The motor, which stalls at 25 N m and turns 45 rpm with no load, drives the crank at full voltage, with its rotor felt through the gearbox as 1061 kg cm2, a 20 ms mechanical time constant.
  4. Pins are frictionless and have no play.
  5. Bodies are rigid, so links do not bend. A long or thin printed link flexes and rings more than this shows.
  6. Contacts are stiff springs that let parts sink into each other by the depth the contact check reports.
  7. Joints and gears have no backlash, and friction acts only where the inputs give it.
