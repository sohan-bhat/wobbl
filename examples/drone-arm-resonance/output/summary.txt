Suggested fixes:
  - Stiffen to move mode 3 above 480 Hz: A natural frequency grows with the square root of stiffness, so mode 3 at 196 Hz needs about 6 times the stiffness to clear the blade pass (2 blades) and the once per turn by 20%. For a beam carrying a heavy end mass that is a section about 1.8 times as deep in the bending direction, and when the beam's own mass dominates about 2.4 times as deep.
  - Or move mode 3 below 69.4 Hz: Softer mounts or isolators that bring it to 0.13 times the stiffness put the mode under the excitation, which works when the speed only passes through it on the way up.
  - Lighten the attached mass: With an end mass dominating, the frequency goes as 1/sqrt(mass), so 2.4 times the frequency needs about 0.17 of the mass.
  - Add damping: At resonance the response is about 1/(2 zeta) of the static one, 16.7 times now. A damping layer or a damped mount that doubles the damping halves it.
