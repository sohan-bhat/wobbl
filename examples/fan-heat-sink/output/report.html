Assumptions

  1. 9 fins 1.5 mm thick and 30 mm tall, gaps of 5.81 mm, 60 mm along the flow.
  2. The fan's air is ducted through the fins with none going around them, as with a shroud or a fan mounted on the end of the sink.
  3. The fan curve is a straight line from its free flow to its shut off pressure.
  4. Air at 40 C warms by 7 C through the sink, so the fins see air at 43.5 C on average.
  5. One heat transfer coefficient from the gap's mean covers every fin and the base between them, the fin tips under the shroud and the outside of the base get 5 W/(m2 K).
  6. Entry and exit losses at the fins come from Kays and London's contraction and expansion coefficients.
  7. Aluminum 6063-T5: conductivity 209 W/(m K), specific heat 900 J/(kg K), density 2700 kg/m3.
  8. Heat enters through bottom and box 17.5,17.5,-1,42.5,42.5,0.5.
  9. Surrounding air or coolant at 43.5 C does not heat up.
  10. Radiation is ignored (conservative).
  11. Contact resistance between parts and internal airflow are not modeled.
