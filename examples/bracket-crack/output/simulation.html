Where does a crack at the pin hole go, and do 99% of brackets last 100,000 cycles?

FAILS

Fixed at bolt holes. Loaded with 4 KN (about 408 kg) acting on the 0 face.

stress

Peak 320 MPa on the 6 mm inside fillet (face 3) at (34.5, -14.2, 3.02), safety factor 1.57 against yield (target 1.5). full report

crack

A 1.27 mm through crack at the edge of the 8 mm hole at (91.7, -3.6, 2.5) fails after about 86,219 cycles in a typical part. One part in ten fails by 55,144 cycles and one in a hundred by 37,968. 68% of parts fail before the 100000 cycle target (allowed 1%). full report
