Assumptions:
  - Linear elastic material and small deflections (no yielding redistribution, no buckling).
  - Polycarbonate: E 2350 MPa, Poisson 0.37, von Mises vs yield 62 MPa (Covestro Makrolon 2405 datasheet).
  - Loads are static and applied all at once; no impact, vibration or fatigue.
  - Walls are shells on their midsurface with the thickness the CAD gives at each point; stress is taken on both faces. Through the thickness stress under a load and at sharp inside corners is not modeled.
  - Fixed faces hold the walls they touch against moving and turning.
  - 4 bosses are beams with their own round or pipe section, joined to the wall they meet by a stiff spider over their outline; their own weight is left out.
  - Rims, edges and chunky features that are not walls are left out of the shell, so their stiffness and stress are not in the model; the solid cross check shows what that costs.
  - Molded part treated as isotropic and uniform; weld lines and the area near the gate can be weaker than the datasheet.
  - Points within 3 mm of supports, boss joints and rigid attachments are left out of the answer.
