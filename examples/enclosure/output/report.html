Assumptions

  1. Linear elastic material and small deflections (no yielding redistribution, no buckling).
  2. Polycarbonate: E 2350 MPa, Poisson 0.37, von Mises vs yield 62 MPa (Covestro Makrolon 2405 datasheet).
  3. Loads are static and applied all at once; no impact, vibration or fatigue.
  4. Walls are shells on their midsurface with the thickness the CAD gives at each point; stress is taken on both faces. Through the thickness stress under a load and at sharp inside corners is not modeled.
  5. Fixed faces hold the walls they touch against moving and turning.
  6. 4 bosses are beams with their own round or pipe section, joined to the wall they meet by a stiff spider over their outline; their own weight is left out.
  7. Rims, edges and chunky features that are not walls are left out of the shell, so their stiffness and stress are not in the model; the solid cross check shows what that costs.
  8. Molded part treated as isotropic and uniform; weld lines and the area near the gate can be weaker than the datasheet.
  9. Points within 3 mm of supports, boss joints and rigid attachments are left out of the answer.

Notes

  • Built from enclosure.py with wall=3, length=90, width=60, height=28, corner=6, boss=7.
  • The shell model holds 92% of the part's material in 21 wall faces.
  • 17 faces that are rims, edges or chunky features such as bosses are not walls and are left out of the shell.
