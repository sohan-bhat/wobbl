Assumptions

  1. Propane (C3H8) and air as O2 + 3.76 N2 at 298 K and 101 kPa, fully mixed.
  2. Adiabatic and at constant pressure, with the products in chemical equilibrium on NASA's thermodynamic data (McBride, Gordon and Reno, NASA TM-4513). A real flame loses heat by radiation and to walls, so its peak runs below this temperature.
  3. Equilibrium NO is an upper bound on what a flame makes, since forming NO takes longer than most flames last.
