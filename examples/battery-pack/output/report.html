How to fix it

Raise heat transfer about 2x
The temperature rise scales with 1/(h A). A small fan takes h from about 5 to 25 W/(m2 K); fins or a larger case add area. Either way you need about 2x the current 5 W/(m2 K) times area.
Cut the power to 3.04 W
Lower current or duty cycle; the rise is proportional to the heat made.

Assumptions

  1. Li-ion cell (lumped, effective): conductivity 3 W/(m K), specific heat 950 J/(kg K), density 2500 kg/m3.
  2. Heat is generated evenly through the part.
  3. Convection uses a fixed film coefficient; real coefficients vary with temperature and airflow.
  4. Surrounding air or coolant at 35 C does not heat up.
  5. Radiation with emissivity 0.9.
  6. Contact resistance between parts and internal airflow are not modeled.
