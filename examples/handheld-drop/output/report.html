How to fix it

A material good for 136 MPa
The worst landing peaks at 68.1 MPa; reaching the 2 target takes a material whose von Mises vs yield strength is at least 136 MPa at a similar stiffness, or a tougher grade that yields instead of cracking.
Soften the landing
A rubber bumper or corner guard lengthens the impact and lowers both the stress and the g. Check it by running the same drop with --floor 'layer:E=5,t=3', a 3 mm elastomer skin.
Spread the load near the worst spot
A rib or a thicker wall around the peak spreads the impact. The stress has to fall by 54% to reach the target.

Assumptions

  1. Wobbl guessed: landing not given, so every face and corner is tried (worst).
  2. Dropped from 1.2 m, so it lands at 4.85 m/s (no air drag), without spin, landing -x-y-z corner down.
  3. Floor: Concrete slab, far stiffer than the part (E about 30 GPa, ACI 318-19 19.2.2.1, Ec = 4700 sqrt(f'c) for 40 MPa concrete).
  4. Polycarbonate: E 2350 MPa, Poisson 0.37, density 1200 kg/m3, von Mises vs yield 62 MPa (Covestro Makrolon 2405 datasheet). Strength at impact rates is often higher than the datasheet's static value, so this errs on the safe side.
  5. Linear elastic part, small strains, no friction with the floor. The first impact only: a bounce and a second landing on another corner are not followed.
  6. Stress is averaged over the time a wave takes to cross two elements, and the accelerometer record is low pass filtered at 10 kHz, the bandwidth of a drop test accelerometer.
  7. The contact patch itself, within two elements of the first mesh of where the part meets the floor, is reported apart: its stress is the local crush of an ideally sharp landing.
  8. 2% of the part's mass is added at the nodes of the smallest elements so they do not hold the time step down (selective mass scaling); the momentum and energy checks include it.
  9. Added mass: 45 g spread over 3916 nodes at 0,0,6 r=26.
  10. Mirror symmetry of the part made these landings the same as one already run: -x face down stands for +x face down; -y face down stands for +y face down; -x-y-z corner down stands for -x+y-z corner down, +x-y-z corner down, +x+y-z corner down; -x-y+z corner down stands for -x+y+z corner down, +x-y+z corner down, +x+y+z corner down.
  11. Predicted run time 26 min against a budget of 50 min, so the mesh was left as meshed. The whole study took 20 min
