Suggested fixes:
  - A material good for 136 MPa: The worst landing peaks at 68.1 MPa; reaching the 2 target takes a material whose von Mises vs yield strength is at least 136 MPa at a similar stiffness, or a tougher grade that yields instead of cracking.
  - Soften the landing: A rubber bumper or corner guard lengthens the impact and lowers both the stress and the g. Check it by running the same drop with --floor 'layer:E=5,t=3', a 3 mm elastomer skin.
  - Spread the load near the worst spot: A rib or a thicker wall around the peak spreads the impact. The stress has to fall by 54% to reach the target.
