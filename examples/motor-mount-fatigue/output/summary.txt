Assumptions:
  - Wobbl guessed: required life not given, so Wobbl assumed 1e7.
  - Stress life method for crack initiation, as Shigley sets it out, with an S-N line from f Sut at 1000 cycles to the corrected endurance strength. It does not cover crack growth after a crack starts, which is a separate damage tolerance question.
  - S-N data for Custom material (cast) give 42.7 MPa at 500 million cycles, smooth specimens, fully reversed (estimated as 0.26 Sut at 5e8 cycles, the ratio of sand cast A356-T6).
  - The Marin factors are surface as-cast (1.00), size 1.05, load 1.00, reliability 90% (0.897), with the part at room temperature.
  - Mean stress by Goodman, with no credit for a compressive mean.
  - Fatigue lives scatter by a factor of three to ten at the same stress, so the life is an estimate and the safety factor on stress is the steadier number.
  - Each block scales the studied load and cycles it between R times its peak and the peak (or at the study's load ratio when the block gives none), and the stress field keeps its shape, as it does for a linear part.

Suggested fixes:
  - Cut the cyclic stress by 49%: A safety factor of 2 needs every stress 1.94 times lower. In bending the stress falls with the square of the wall, so about 1.39 times the thickness at the peak gets there.
  - Switch to Aluminum 6061-T6: Its fatigue strength is 2.3 times that of Custom material (cast), which covers the 1.94 needed if the part is otherwise the same. It is a wrought alloy, so the part would be made that way instead.
