How to fix it

Cut the cyclic stress by 49%
A safety factor of 2 needs every stress 1.94 times lower. In bending the stress falls with the square of the wall, so about 1.39 times the thickness at the peak gets there.
Switch to Aluminum 6061-T6
Its fatigue strength is 2.3 times that of Custom material (cast), which covers the 1.94 needed if the part is otherwise the same. It is a wrought alloy, so the part would be made that way instead.

Assumptions

  1. Wobbl guessed: required life not given, so Wobbl assumed 1e7.
  2. Stress life method for crack initiation, as Shigley sets it out, with an S-N line from f Sut at 1000 cycles to the corrected endurance strength. It does not cover crack growth after a crack starts, which is a separate damage tolerance question.
  3. S-N data for Custom material (cast) give 42.7 MPa at 500 million cycles, smooth specimens, fully reversed (estimated as 0.26 Sut at 5e8 cycles, the ratio of sand cast A356-T6).
  4. The Marin factors are surface as-cast (1.00), size 1.05, load 1.00, reliability 90% (0.897), with the part at room temperature.
  5. Mean stress by Goodman, with no credit for a compressive mean.
  6. Fatigue lives scatter by a factor of three to ten at the same stress, so the life is an estimate and the safety factor on stress is the steadier number.
  7. Each block scales the studied load and cycles it between R times its peak and the peak (or at the study's load ratio when the block gives none), and the stress field keeps its shape, as it does for a linear part.
