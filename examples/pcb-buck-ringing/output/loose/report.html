Assumptions

  1. Stackup: kicad stackup.
  2. Quasi static TEM cross sections of each trace with its planes; solder mask, plating and surface roughness are left out, and loss is only the DC resistance of the copper.
  3. Crosstalk is for a 1 ns edge on the aggressor.
  4. KiCad 8 board, file format version 20240108.
  5. Parasitics joined 9 of 11 pad connections and 4 net capacitances to the schematic buck.cir, each pad hanging off its net's main chip pad through the copper between them.
  6. The parts are modeled as the netlist gives them. Ideal switches and diodes without reverse recovery ring longer than real ones, whose losses damp the ringing.
