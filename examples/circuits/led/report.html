How to fix it

Use a 91 ohm resistor
Gives about 19.8 mA; it dissipates 35.6 mW, so a 0.25 W part is enough.

Assumptions

  1. blue LED modeled with the diode equation: 3.2 V at 20 mA, ideality 2.7, series resistance 1.5 ohm.
  2. Resistor 47 ohm exact, rated 0.25 W.
  3. Room temperature, steady DC.
