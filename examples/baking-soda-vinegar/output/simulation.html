reaction

CH3COOH(aq) + NaHCO3(s) -> CH3COONa(aq) + H2O(l) + CO2(g) takes in 31.1 kJ per mole as written and goes forward on its own at 298 K (dG = -13.3 kJ, K = 214). At equilibrium 99.5% of it reacts at 1.01 bar. In 1201 g of vinegar at 5% it cools the solution by 6.2 K. It gives off 24.5 L of gas per mole at 298 K and 101 kPa. full report
