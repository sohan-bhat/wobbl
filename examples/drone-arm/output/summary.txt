  - Nylon PA12 (SLS or MJF): E 1700 MPa, Poisson 0.39, von Mises vs yield 48 MPa (HP 3D High Reusability PA 12 datasheet; EOS PA 2200).

  - Printed part treated as isotropic at 100% infill; across the layers it is roughly 90% as strong. Pass --print-direction to check that.

  - Contact between separate parts, friction and preload are not modeled.

Suggested fixes:
  - Stress piles up in the sharp inside corner. A fillet of 1.43 mm (about half the 2.86 mm wall) spreads it out. With that radius the peak is usually about 1.4x the 7.11 MPa beam stress, roughly 9.95 MPa (safety factor 4.82).
  - Stress stays about the same for the same shape and load, so PLA (3D printed) (50 MPa) gives safety factor 4.99. It is 2.1x as stiff, so deflection scales by 0.49. Other options: PETG (3D printed) (4.69), ABS (3D printed) (3.30).
