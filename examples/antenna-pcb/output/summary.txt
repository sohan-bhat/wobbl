Assumptions:
  - Fed by a lumped port, a 0.5 mm wide strip across the 0.6 mm gap from (-9, 3.6, 1.583) to (-9, 3, 1.583) mm carrying 1 A. The impedance is the voltage across the gap over that current, so it holds the gap's capacitance and the strip's inductance.
  - Metals and copper sheets are perfect conductors, so conductor loss is left out. Dielectric loss comes from each material's loss tangent.
  - Free space around the antenna, closed by a sphere of 108 mm radius that absorbs outgoing waves (a second order boundary condition). The far field comes from the fields on a sphere of 66.7 mm.
  - First order edge elements no larger than a tenth of the shortest wavelength in air, one mesh for the whole sweep with 72624 unknowns.
  - 'board' is board laminate, relative permittivity 4.4, loss tangent 0.02.
  - Solved in 3D. The antenna is the geometry in ifa.kicad_pcb, which no closed form describes.
  - The antenna is net ANT. Ground is GND.
  - The board is a slab of relative permittivity 4.4 and loss tangent 0.02, 1.6 mm thick (kicad stackup).
  - Vias are not modeled and copper is a perfect conductor of zero thickness.
