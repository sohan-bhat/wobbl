Assumptions

  1. Fed by a lumped port, a 0.5 mm wide strip across the 0.6 mm gap from (-9, 3.6, 1.583) to (-9, 3, 1.583) mm carrying 1 A. The impedance is the voltage across the gap over that current, so it holds the gap's capacitance and the strip's inductance.
  2. Metals and copper sheets are perfect conductors, so conductor loss is left out. Dielectric loss comes from each material's loss tangent.
  3. Free space around the antenna, closed by a sphere of 108 mm radius that absorbs outgoing waves (a second order boundary condition). The far field comes from the fields on a sphere of 66.7 mm.
  4. First order edge elements no larger than a tenth of the shortest wavelength in air, one mesh for the whole sweep with 72624 unknowns.
  5. 'board' is board laminate, relative permittivity 4.4, loss tangent 0.02.
  6. Solved in 3D. The antenna is the geometry in ifa.kicad_pcb, which no closed form describes.
  7. The antenna is net ANT. Ground is GND.
  8. The board is a slab of relative permittivity 4.4 and loss tangent 0.02, 1.6 mm thick (kicad stackup).
  9. Vias are not modeled and copper is a perfect conductor of zero thickness.
