# Check a part's strength

This guide now lives on the website, at
[sohanbhat.me/wobbl/guides/simulations/strength](https://sohanbhat.me/wobbl/guides/simulations/strength/).

A strength study meshes the part, solves it with CalculiX, refines the mesh around the peaks
until the governing stress settles and then runs its checks, and the verdict is holds, marginal
or fails against the material's limit and your target safety factor. The inputs have pages of
their own for [geometry](https://sohanbhat.me/wobbl/guides/inputs/geometry/),
[loads](https://sohanbhat.me/wobbl/guides/inputs/loads/),
[supports](https://sohanbhat.me/wobbl/guides/inputs/supports/),
[materials](https://sohanbhat.me/wobbl/guides/inputs/materials/), the
[face selectors](https://sohanbhat.me/wobbl/guides/inputs/faces/) and
[units](https://sohanbhat.me/wobbl/guides/inputs/units/).
