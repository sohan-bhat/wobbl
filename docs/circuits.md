# Circuits

This guide now lives on the website, at
[sohan-bhat.github.io/wobbl/guides/simulations/circuits](https://sohan-bhat.github.io/wobbl/guides/simulations/circuits/).

`wobbl circuit` checks an LED, an RC network, a buck converter or a MOSFET in ngspice and compares
each answer with the textbook, and it runs any netlist you give it with the signals you probe.
A blue LED on 5 V through 47 ohm fries at 36.9 mA, and Wobbl suggests 91 ohm instead. Every
option is on the [wobbl circuit](https://sohan-bhat.github.io/wobbl/guides/cli/circuit/) page.
