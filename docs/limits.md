# Limits

This page now lives on the website, at
[sohan-bhat.github.io/wobbl/validation/limits](https://sohan-bhat.github.io/wobbl/validation/limits/).

Wobbl models linear elastic materials under static loads, one material per study, with rigid
wall contact and bolted holes without preload, and motion as a point mass in a vertical plane. Fatigue, impact, vibration, buckling and contact
between separate parts are not modeled yet, and thin walled parts are slow until shell elements
arrive. Shell elements, fatigue estimates and bolt preload are planned for 0.2, CFD with
OpenFOAM for 0.3, and multibody mechanisms with MuJoCo for 0.4.
