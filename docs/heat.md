# Heat

This guide now lives on the website, at
[sohan-bhat.github.io/wobbl/guides/simulations/heat](https://sohan-bhat.github.io/wobbl/guides/simulations/heat/).

`wobbl thermal` solves steady and transient heat with CalculiX, with heat made in the part or
put on chosen faces, convection, radiation and held temperatures. A heat sink carrying a 15 W
chip in a 40 C cabinet reaches 70.3 C, and a battery pack in a sealed box passes 60 C after about
21 minutes. Every option is on the
[wobbl thermal](https://sohan-bhat.github.io/wobbl/guides/cli/thermal/) page.
